How Smart Negotiation & Commission Work

Transparent workflows aligned with our Telegram bot core engine.

1. Free Listing & Smart Negotiation

Providers list assets/services at zero cost. Seekers and providers negotiate prices using the smart deals module to reach an agreement.

2. Commission Payment Only

No direct asset payments are made on the platform. The platform only collects the platform commission for viewing details and contact numbers, prompted by:

"To view details and contact information, please select a payment method below to pay the commission."
3. Contact Dispatch & In-Person Finalization

Upon commission payment, contact info is dispatched. Final payments and delivery happen in person:

  • Seeker: "Directly contact the provider to arrange delivery. Thank you for using Smart Match Africa!"
  • Provider: "Expect their call shortly, or contact them directly to get started. Thank you for using Smart Match Africa!"

📜 Platform Terms, Policies & Commission Framework

1. 100% Free Listing Model for Providers

Asset providers (Housing sales/rentals, Vehicle sales/rentals, Equipment rentals, Ride-hailing drivers, and Freight carriers) can list, sell, or rent out their assets at zero cost. Buyers or renters agree to pay the platform service commission.

2. Smart Negotiations & Commission-Only Payments

No direct asset or logistics payments are processed on the platform. The platform exclusively collects the platform commission for viewing details and contact numbers.
Guidance Prompt: "To view details and contact information, please select a payment method below to pay the commission."
The smart deals module enables parties to negotiate prices and reach a mutual agreement prior to commission payment.

3. Contact Dispatch & In-Person Finalization

Upon successful commission payment, contact information is securely dispatched:

  • For Seeker: "Directly contact the provider to arrange delivery. Thank you for using Smart Match Africa!"
  • For Provider: "Expect their call shortly, or contact them directly to get started. Thank you for using Smart Match Africa!"

All final asset or service payments are completed securely in person between the parties.
